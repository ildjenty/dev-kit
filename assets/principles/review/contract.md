# Contracts

Review how the contract accepts states and values, how it classifies failures, and what it returns publicly.

## Criteria

Principle 3 of `principles/data.md` ("Prevent acceptance of invalid states and values with types and validation") and principle 1 of `principles/engineering.md` ("Do not hide failures") are part of the criteria.

## What to check

- **Accepting states and values**: Follow principle 3 of `principles/data.md`.
- **Failure classification and public responses**: Follow principle 1 of `principles/engineering.md`.

## Blocking

A blocking condition of either principle applies.

## Exemptions

The exemptions defined in these principles apply.
