# Irreversible items

Review the list of irreversible items, the decisions that are difficult to change, for omissions and for when each is decided and finalized.

## Criteria

Principle 1 of `principles/designing.md` ("Identify difficult-to-change decisions first and finalize them after obtaining decision material") is part of the criteria.

## What to check

Check the list of irreversible items for omissions. Follow principle 1 of `principles/designing.md` for when to decide and finalize them.

## Blocking

The list has an omission, or a blocking condition of principle 1 of `principles/designing.md` applies.

## Exemptions

The exemption defined in principle 1 of `principles/designing.md` applies.
