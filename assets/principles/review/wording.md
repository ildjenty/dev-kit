# Wording

Review the assumptions about the intended reader and the specificity of the wording.

## Criteria

Principles 2 ("Define the intended reader's assumptions and write without depending on the conversation") and 3 ("Write with concrete words that show who does what") of `principles/writing.md` are part of the criteria.

## What to check

Check assumptions about the intended reader and the specificity of wording against the violations and warning signs in principles 2 and 3 of `principles/writing.md`.

## Blocking

A passage causes the reader to misunderstand its meaning.

## Exemptions

The exemptions defined in principles 2 and 3 of `principles/writing.md` apply.
