# structure

- Checks: Placement: Check whether each placement can be justified by its responsibility. Principles: Check for violations or warning signs under the principles in `principles/designing.md`, `principles/structure.md`, and `principles/data.md`. Simplicity: Follow principle 7 of `principles/structure.md` (`principles/structure.md#7-reduce-layers-to-traverse-and-state-to-remember`) for comparing alternatives. Boundaries: Follow principle 2 of `principles/structure.md` (`principles/structure.md#2-do-not-repeat-validation-while-its-validity-conditions-remain-true`) for validating external inputs and principle 5 of `principles/data.md` (`principles/data.md#5-define-data-ownership-and-lifetime-and-the-boundaries-that-protect-invariants`) for consistency during updates. Style: Follow principle 7 of `principles/designing.md` (`principles/designing.md#7-follow-existing-conventions`) for aligning structure.
- blocking: A blocking condition in a referenced principle applies.
- Do not report: Items outside this aspect's checks. Findings without a failure scenario.
- Output format: Follow the Judgment JSON Schema supplied in the request (`schemas/judgment.json`) and `schemas/finding.json`, with the judgment rules in `checks/README.md`. Return `NOT ASSESSED` when necessary criteria or required verification are unavailable, and record the verified and unverified scope, method, and environment. Do not return findings without a failure scenario, and do not assign severity. Retain all findings; treat nonblocking findings as reference information without requiring fixes. Compare exemption claims with their grounds, and waive only the requirements covered by the exemption.
