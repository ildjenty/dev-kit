# completion

- Checks: Check the termination block (`schemas/progress.json`, a record returning work results in a fixed format) for the round (from work instruction to the decision on its result) included in the judgment request against the completion conditions in the plan (`schemas/plan.json`) and the handoff requirements for interrupted work in principle 5 of `principles/context.md` (`principles/context.md#5-meet-completion-criteria-before-finishing-and-leave-interrupted-work-safe-to-resume`). Leave aggregation of required checks and confirmation of commit success to devkit's completion finalization under the completion conditions (`stages/README.md#completion-finalization`).
- blocking: Follow the blocking conditions of principle 5. Follow the same principle when required verification remains outstanding.
- Do not report: Items outside this aspect's checks. Findings without a failure scenario.
- Output format: Follow the Judgment JSON Schema supplied in the request (`schemas/judgment.json`) and `schemas/finding.json`, with the judgment rules in `checks/README.md`. Return `NOT ASSESSED` when necessary criteria or required verification are unavailable, and record the verified and unverified scope, method, and environment. Do not return findings without a failure scenario, and do not assign severity. Retain all findings; treat nonblocking findings as reference information without requiring fixes. Compare exemption claims with their grounds, and waive only the requirements covered by the exemption.
