# harness

- Checks: Principles and contracts: Follow the principles in `principles/harness.md` and the applicable schemas and runtime rules in `schemas/`, `stages/README.md`, `checks/README.md`, and `plans/README.md`. Required checks: Follow principle 5 for selecting mechanisms and the detection test requirements (`checks/README.md#detection-tests`, tests of whether aspect files produce the expected judgments) for making an aspect handled by a judge (`schemas/plan.json`, the role responsible for a check) required. Skill: Check for a `description` (`https://agentskills.io/specification#description-field`, the skill description field), placement (the canonical devkit skill is `.agents/skills/devkit/`, linked from `.claude/skills/devkit`), and references. Follow principle 5 of `principles/working.md` for dividing work between implementation and procedures. Judgment: Check whether `NOT ASSESSED` can be returned under the check results contract (`schemas/judgment.json` and `checks/README.md`). Principle content: Check whether applicability, requirements, exceptions, and blocking conditions can be understood. Fresh session: Check whether reading the repository and execution records answers what the system is, how to run it, how to verify it, and where the work currently stands. Retrospective: When assessing a retrospective, follow `stages/retrospective.md` and check that the review considers all judgments and findings, including nonblocking findings, FAIL and NOT ASSESSED outcomes, round counts, human rejections, corrections and replanning instructions, missed issues reported as lessons through note, and the norms inventory. For each problem, check where it should have been caught and the destination for preventing recurrence, following `[lessons] destinations` in `devkit.toml` (default order: machine checks such as hooks, lint, types, tests and verification commands; plan templates; aspects; principles; skill). Check that repository-owned norms are updated, or that a reason for no update is recorded; changed aspects need updated detection examples. Consider unenforced items in `rules.md` and recurring violations as candidates for machine checks. Check that approved completion conditions constituting repository requirements are reflected in `requirements.md` unless already covered by purpose, and that decisions needed by later work are retained in `requirements.md`, `structure.json`, or `rules.md` as appropriate rather than only in execution records. Norms and burden: Check how relevant principles are used by aspects and plan templates, whether mechanisms serve their purpose, and whether any mechanism is unused or costs more than it warrants. Check for simpler alternatives to the number and format of components, principles, records, and aspects under principle 5. Focus on problems, misses, gaps, and burdens observed in this work; require a full norms inventory review only when the work shows a need.
- blocking: A fresh session cannot answer one of the questions, a blocking condition in a referenced principle applies, or a referenced contract is violated.
- Do not report: Items outside this aspect's checks. Findings without a failure scenario.
- Output format: Follow the Judgment JSON Schema supplied in the request (`schemas/judgment.json`) and `schemas/finding.json`, with the judgment rules in `checks/README.md`. Return `NOT ASSESSED` when necessary criteria or required verification are unavailable, and record the verified and unverified scope, method, and environment. Do not return findings without a failure scenario, and do not assign severity. Retain all findings; treat nonblocking findings as reference information without requiring fixes. Compare exemption claims with their grounds, and waive only the requirements covered by the exemption.
