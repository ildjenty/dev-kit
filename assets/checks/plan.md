# plan

- Checks: Gates: Check that reasons are given as required by the plan template (`schemas/plan-template.json` and `plans/README.md`), and that decisions to ask a human follow the human confirmation requirements (`router/README.md` and `plans/README.md`). Deviations: Check deviations from the plan template (`schemas/plan.json` and `plans/README.md`) and whether applicable findings are marked `needs-decision` (`schemas/finding.json`, a finding requiring a human decision). Size: Check whether stages can be reduced. Coverage: Check whether each completion condition has a corresponding check and whether the inspections needed for the change type (UI, data, external communication, or structure) are included.
- blocking: A completion condition has no corresponding check.
- Do not report: Items outside this aspect's checks. Findings without a failure scenario.
- Output format: Follow the Judgment JSON Schema supplied in the request (`schemas/judgment.json`) and `schemas/finding.json`, with the judgment rules in `checks/README.md`. Return `NOT ASSESSED` when necessary criteria or required verification are unavailable, and record the verified and unverified scope, method, and environment. Do not return findings without a failure scenario, and do not assign severity. Retain all findings; treat nonblocking findings as reference information without requiring fixes. Compare exemption claims with their grounds, and waive only the requirements covered by the exemption.
