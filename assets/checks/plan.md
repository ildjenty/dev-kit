# plan

- Checks: Request coverage: Compare the fixed original user request (when observed) and the task supplied to start with the completion conditions and `open_questions`. Preserve every requested requirement; do not drop requirements or invent requirements absent from the request. Record ambiguity in `open_questions` rather than guessing. When the original request was not observed, state that limitation and use the supplied task without treating it as the original. Gates: Check that reasons are given as required by the plan template (`schemas/plan-template.json` and `plans/README.md`), and that human confirmation follows `plans/README.md` and the approval policy in `devkit.toml`. Under the default policy, the first plan, a plan changing completion condition IDs or wording from the last human-approved version, a plan with deviations, and a plan declaring external operations require human approval; replanning with the same conditions and no deviations, external operations, or open questions does not require renewed approval. A nonempty `open_questions` array always requires human approval, regardless of policy. Deviations: Check deviations from the plan template, including omitted stages (`schemas/plan.json` and `plans/README.md`), and whether applicable findings are marked `needs-decision` (`schemas/finding.json`, a finding requiring a human decision). Unresolved requirements: Check that the planner records requirements it cannot decide in `open_questions` rather than filling them by assumption. Split work: Check whether unit scopes overlap and report a concrete collision scenario. Unit IDs are unique within the plan. Adding or removing split is a template deviation; stages already completed in this work and omitted by replanning are not removed-stage deviations. Size: Check whether stages can be reduced. Inspections: Check whether the inspections needed for the change type (UI, data, external communication, or structure) are included. Verification: Check that each completion condition has a way to verify it: a check run by a verification command, a judge, or a judgment command, or a human gate. Checks are not necessarily mechanical; conditions about agreeing on requirements are verified by judges and human gates.
- blocking: The plan drops or invents requirements, bypasses required human approval, treats an unresolved requirement as agreed without a human decision, leaves a completion condition with no way to verify it, or gives units overlapping scopes that can cause conflicting changes.
- Do not report: Items outside this aspect's checks. Findings without a failure scenario.
- Output format: Follow the Judgment JSON Schema supplied in the request (`schemas/judgment.json`) and `schemas/finding.json`, with the judgment rules in `checks/README.md`. Return `NOT ASSESSED` when necessary criteria or required verification are unavailable, and record the verified and unverified scope, method, and environment. Do not return findings without a failure scenario, and do not assign severity. Retain all findings; treat nonblocking findings as reference information without requiring fixes. Compare exemption claims with their grounds, and waive only the requirements covered by the exemption.
