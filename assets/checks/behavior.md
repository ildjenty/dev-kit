# behavior

- Checks: General: Check startup, interaction, console errors, evidence capture, and cleanup. Data changes: Check whether saved content and retrieved results match the specification. External communication changes: Check whether sent and received content and failure handling match the specification. UI changes: Follow the screen rules (`rules.md#screens`). Follow principle 9 of `principles/ui.md` (`principles/ui.md#9-check-rendered-results-and-interactions`) for checking rendering and interaction, including data prone to problems, such as long names, no results, large datasets, and multiple languages.
- blocking: Saved content, retrieved results, sent or received content, or communication failure handling differs from the specification. For UI changes, the blocking conditions of principle 9 or violations of the screen rules are also blocking. Follow the same principle for unverified items.
- Do not report: Items outside this aspect's checks. Findings without a failure scenario.
- Output format: Follow the Judgment JSON Schema supplied in the request (`schemas/judgment.json`) and `schemas/finding.json`, with the judgment rules in `checks/README.md`. Return `NOT ASSESSED` when necessary criteria or required verification are unavailable, and record the verified and unverified scope, method, and environment. Do not return findings without a failure scenario, and do not assign severity. Retain all findings; treat nonblocking findings as reference information without requiring fixes. Compare exemption claims with their grounds, and waive only the requirements covered by the exemption.
