# Decision authority

## Decisions to raise with the human

- Difficult-to-change or wide-reaching choices: technologies, architecture, data models, public interfaces, module boundaries, and dependency direction.
- Decisions not settled by requirements, external contracts, or norms that affect completion conditions or externally visible behavior.
- Departures from norms.
- Decisions affecting cost, operations, or security.

## Delegated decisions

None recorded. Add decision kinds the human has explicitly allowed without approval, with grounds from human input or question and answer record IDs. These delegations take precedence over overlapping categories above.
