# Reports

Review PR bodies and reports.

## Criteria

The following are part of the criteria:

- Principle 1 of `writing.md`, "Put conclusions and information needed for human decisions first."
- Principle 1 of `verification.md`, "Do not present unverified matters as verified."
- The PR body format specified by the workflow's writing instructions and report assembly.

## What to check

- **PR body**: Follow the PR body format specified by the workflow's writing instructions and report assembly.
- **Reporting**: Follow principle 1 of `writing.md` for information needed for decisions and principle 1 of `verification.md` for verification claims. Apply both to PR bodies too.
- **Comparison with the diff and judgments**: Check that reported changes, verification results, and decisions match the diff and judgment records.

## Blocking

A blocking condition of a principle named above applies.

## Exemptions

The exemptions defined in the principles named above apply.
