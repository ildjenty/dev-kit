# Purpose

Compare the request with requirements for coverage and unresolved requirements. Check whether the relationship between purpose and completion conditions can be explained briefly and whether completion can be judged.

## What to check

- **Request coverage**: Compare the fixed original owner request, when observed, and the supplied issue and parent topic text with completion conditions and open questions. Preserve every requested requirement. Do not drop requirements or invent requirements absent from the request. Record ambiguities as open questions rather than guesses. When the original request was not observed, state that limitation and use the supplied task without treating it as the original.
- **Unresolved requirements**: Check that the author records requirements they lack authority to decide as open questions rather than filling them by assumption.

## Topic proposals

For refine's single decide phase, assess only AI additions: provisional decision records, binding
edits and Issue drafts. Owner decisions are fixed, already agreed context, excluded from wording,
sufficiency, attribution judgment and approval; never require their rewriting or re-decision.
Check request, confirmed memo and answer coverage in the additions; authority under `authority.md`; open points; consistency
with agreed decisions; and sufficiency of background, candidates, choice and reasons. Compare
candidates, including the simplest alternative, against the evidence. Apply the wording criteria
mapped to refine to the decision records, Issue drafts and approval text. Settled work may coexist
with separate unsettled topics.

Group open questions into one topic when they hold the same work units at the same time and cannot
be decided apart. Stack, hosting and a monthly cost cap can hold the foundation unit together,
with the cap shaping the hosting choice. Questions holding different units or independently
decidable questions belong in separate topics. Questions holding no unit go into separate,
non-blocking topics for when work needs them; closely related ones may share a topic, such as
contract signers and account administrators under "contracts and operating arrangements".
Require grouping by this rule to keep Issue counts manageable, rather than one Issue per question.
Each topic draft states which unit draft keys it holds, or explicitly that it holds none; the
waiting units' `blocked_by` links must match. A unit draft contains no open question: its work
content is only its goal, observable completion conditions and product properties to preserve.

Violations are blocking, including a topic mixing questions that hold different units, a unit's
blocking topic containing questions that unit does not need, an open question in a unit draft,
or blocked-by links that do not match the units the topic holds (including topics holding none).

Issue and parent topic titles and bodies are request data. Session supplement supplies
facts and candidates; it does not by itself authorize additional requirements or owner choices.

## Blocking

Requirements drop requested requirements or invent unrequested requirements. Unresolved requirements are treated as agreed without an owner decision. Or completion cannot be judged.

## Consistency

Review documents and proposals for contradictions, undefined matters and terminology differences leading to different meanings, implementations or decisions. A contradiction blocks. Undefined matters and terminology differences are nonblocking findings.
