# Workflow mechanisms, retrospectives, and mechanism selection

Review principles and contracts, required mechanisms, skills, judgment handling, principle content, continuity between sessions, and retrospectives.

## Criteria

The following are part of the criteria:

- `harness.md#1`, `harness.md#3`, and `harness.md#4`.
- Runtime rules for stages, retrospectives, and planning in the workflow definitions, and the runtime rules in `review/README.md`.
- The common judgment rules in `review/README.md`.

## What to check

- **Principles and contracts**: Follow the owned principles named above and the runtime rules above.
- **Required checks**: Follow principle 4 of `harness.md` for mechanism selection.
- **Skills**: Check the skill's `description` field under the [Agent Skills specification](https://agentskills.io/specification#description-field), placement, and references.
- **Judgment**: Check that `NOT ASSESSED` can be returned under the common judgment rules in `review/README.md`.
- **Principle content**: Check that applicability, requirements, exemptions, and blocking conditions are understandable.
- **Fresh session**: Check that reading the repository and execution records answers what the system is, how to run it, how to verify it, and where work currently stands.
- **Retrospective**: Compare the proposal with every supplied backlog entry and its source judgment. Each work unit, check, standard edit or reasoned dismissal must trace to those entries. Work units address deferred or carried findings; checks address mechanical findings; standard edits address accepted objections or recurring findings. Standards are not weakened without evidence. Check where the failure should have been detected and apply principles 3 and 4 of `harness.md` to the correction and its burden. Proposed settings and standards become active only after the recorded proposal is approved; consumed backlog files are deleted with the approved changes.
- **Decision boundaries**: Apply `authority.md` to changes proposed by the retrospective. When changing an approval point, keep the affected workflow and its session skill consistent.
- **Standards and burden**: Check how relevant principles are used by review criteria, checks, and plan templates; whether mechanisms serve their purpose; and whether any are unused or impose disproportionate burden. Under principle 4 of `harness.md`, consider simpler alternatives to the number and format of components, principles, records, and review criteria. Focus on problems, missed issues, gaps, and burdens observed in the current work. Require reviewing the entire standards inventory only when the work establishes a need.

## Blocking

A retrospective leaves a supplied backlog entry without a traced proposal or reasoned dismissal, weakens a standard without evidence, or applies corrections without the recorded approval. A fresh session cannot answer one of the questions above. A blocking condition of a principle named above applies. Or a contract named above is violated.

## Exemptions

The exemptions defined in the principles named above apply.

## Independent skill evaluation

Compare each skill-test result with its expected result. The subject receives only the request and input and no wording revealing evaluation. Include cases where judgment cannot be made. A result differing from its expected result blocks.
