# Scope

Which changes fulfill the agreed request and preserve affected uses within authority. Read the principle references in the supplied materials; their applicability and exemptions remain in force.

## Decisions

No plan judge: this quality compares the implemented change with the agreed request.

## Code

Check conformance to requirements, decisions and owner answers: the change does what was asked and nothing more. Compare all changed operations and decisions with valid approvals and delegations under `authority.md`. Investigate affected uses and errors with the same cause under `engineering.md#5`. Apply `working.md#6` and `working.md#7` to scope and execution. Compare structural differences with approved decisions; a reference pattern alone is no constraint. A choice passed at plan time is checked for conformance, not judged again as a choice. Without a plan, assess the supplied `without_plan` choices using Decisions and `every_quality`.

## Blocking

An owned principle blocking condition applies; required owner approval is bypassed; a finalized owner decision lacks its matching answer or approval; an unresolved owner matter is neither decided nor proposed at the current approval point; later work departs from committed approved decisions; an assigned condition is unmet. A blocking condition of the supplied `every_quality` principles also applies to this quality’s rows.

## Not reported

Out-of-scope cleanup, different reference-pattern divisions without a failure, or decisions already settled by owner answers.
