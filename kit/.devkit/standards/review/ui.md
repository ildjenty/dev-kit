# Ui

How users understand and complete their task through visible states and interactions. Read the principle references in the supplied materials; their applicability and exemptions remain in force.

## Decisions

Check the user purpose, familiar concepts, whole-task burden, information priority and wording, recovery, states and transitions, accessibility, numeric comparisons and approval versus notification under the owned principles. Apply `every_quality` to your own rows: identify omitted hard-to-change choices made by the skeleton and check the timing and evidence of finalization under `designing.md#1`. Check decision assignments and approval against `authority.md`.

## Code

Check conformance to each UI row. Verify rendered results and interactions, startup, console errors, evidence capture and cleanup under `ui.md#9`, including long names, no results, large datasets and multiple languages. Compare demonstrations with assigned completion condition IDs and ensure correction rounds stay within the approved design and requirements. A choice passed at plan time is checked for conformance, not judged again as a choice. Without a plan, assess the supplied `without_plan` choices using Decisions and `every_quality`.

## Blocking

A blocking condition of an owned principle applies, an assigned UI condition is not demonstrated, or a correction absorbs an unapproved requirement or design change. A blocking condition of the supplied `every_quality` principles also applies to this quality’s rows.

## Not reported

Preferences beyond the agreed design or additional interactions unrelated to the request.
