# Document

How delivered documents let their intended readers understand, decide and perform the described work. Read the principle references in the supplied materials; their applicability and exemptions remain in force.

## Decisions

No plan judge: this quality assesses the delivered documents.

## Code

Read changed READMEs, guides, specifications, design documents and user-facing text files in the judged tree. Apply `writing.md#2` to reader assumptions, terms, references and explanations that stand without the conversation, and `writing.md#3` to concrete actors, actions and criteria. Use `writing.md#4` for enough structure and examples to perform the work, `writing.md#5` for authoritative sources, update ownership and consistency, and `writing.md#6` to distinguish current specifications from history and plans. Apply `writing.md#8` to diagrams and `writing.md#9` to disclosure to the intended readers. Compare instructions and claims with the supplied requirements and relevant source files; name the passage and evidence.

Check conformance to passed plan choices without choosing them again. Refine proposals and PR bodies are assessed by their own judges; this quality assesses documents delivered in the change.

## Blocking

A blocking condition of an owned principle applies. Contradictory instructions or claims about the same current behavior also block when they lead readers to different implementations or actions. Identify the concrete misunderstanding, failed action or unauthorized disclosure and its evidence.

## Not reported

Personal preferences for length, headings, word choice or diagrams when readers can understand and perform the work. Historical text clearly identified as history, and exemptions whose conditions the document demonstrates. Do not report an optional example or diagram merely because it is absent.
