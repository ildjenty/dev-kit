# completion

- Checks: Check the termination block (`schemas/progress.json`, a record returning work results in a fixed format) for the round (from work instruction to the decision on its result) included in the judgment request against the completion conditions in the plan (`schemas/plan.json`) and the handoff requirements for interrupted work in principle 5 of `principles/context.md` (`principles/context.md#5-%E5%AE%8C%E4%BA%86%E6%9D%A1%E4%BB%B6%E3%82%92%E6%BA%80%E3%81%9F%E3%81%97%E3%81%A6%E7%B5%82%E3%81%88%E4%B8%AD%E6%96%AD%E6%99%82%E3%81%AF%E5%AE%89%E5%85%A8%E3%81%AB%E5%86%8D%E9%96%8B%E3%81%A7%E3%81%8D%E3%82%8B%E7%8A%B6%E6%85%8B%E3%82%92%E6%AE%8B%E3%81%99`). Leave aggregation of required checks and confirmation of commit success to devkit's completion finalization under the completion conditions (`stages/README.md#completion-finalization`).
- blocking: Follow the blocking conditions of principle 5. Follow the same principle when required verification remains outstanding.
- Do not report: Items outside this aspect's checks. Findings without a failure scenario.
- Output format: Follow the Judgment JSON Schema supplied in the request (`schemas/judgment.json`) and `schemas/finding.json`, with the judgment rules in `checks/README.md`. Return `NOT ASSESSED` when necessary criteria or required verification are unavailable, and record the verified and unverified scope, method, and environment. Do not return findings without a failure scenario, and do not assign severity. Retain all findings; treat nonblocking findings as reference information without requiring fixes. Compare exemption claims with their grounds, and waive only the requirements covered by the exemption.
